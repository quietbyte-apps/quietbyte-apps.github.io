TowerTap Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

TowerTap ("the app") shows information about the cell tower your phone is connected to and can reset your phone's mobile radio. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

Summary

TowerTap does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send data off your device. It contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Location (precise and approximate): Android only reveals cell tower identity (cell ID, eNB/gNB, band, signal strength) to apps that hold location permission. TowerTap uses it only to read cell tower information. It does not read GPS coordinates.
  • Phone state: Used to read the network type (LTE/5G), the 5G display status and which SIM handles mobile data. TowerTap does not read your phone number, call log or contacts.
  • Notifications: Used to show the live tower notification and tower-change and weak-signal alerts.
  • Foreground service (location): When you turn on Background monitor, a visible notification keeps tower monitoring running. You can stop it at any time.
  • Accessibility service (optional): If you turn on "TowerTap radio reset", the app uses the Accessibility Service API only to switch "Mobile Radio Power" off and back on in the system Radio Info screen of the Phone app, and only for 20 seconds after you request a reset. It does not read, collect or store any other screen content or personal data. The app shows a disclosure and asks for your consent before you are taken to turn it on.

Data stored on your device

TowerTap keeps a log of the towers you have connected to (tower ID, band, signal range, time) and your settings in the app's private storage on your phone. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it with the Export button, which hands a CSV file to an app you pick through Android's share sheet. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
